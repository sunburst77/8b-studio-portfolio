About the piece

A birthday commission for a software lead, built from twelve reference photos. Every detail came from her own desk — the code on the monitor, the drawer unit, the grey runners she wears every day. Sculpted in 34 hours, painted by hand over four days.

About 8B-Studio

8B-Studio is a small team of sculptors, painters and print technicians. We make one-of-one collectible figures of real people — founders, families, teams, partners — capturing the posture, the outfit and the small habits that make someone recognisable at a glance.
